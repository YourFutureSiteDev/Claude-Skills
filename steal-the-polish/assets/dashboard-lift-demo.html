4. Take and leave

Take: Doto numbers Free Google font. Numbers only, never body text.
Take: glow tiles Pure CSS. Two or three per screen or it turns into a lolly shop.
Take: ghost bars Anywhere you show target vs actual.
Leave: the forecast candle chart Trading desk styling. A tradie will not know what it means.
