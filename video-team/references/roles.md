## What "replica" means here

Roles 1 to 3 encode how three specific people are documented to actually work,
in as much detail as the public record supports: the leaked onboarding document
Jimmy Donaldson wrote for his own staff, Paddy Galloway's own strategy posts and
long-form interviews, and two independent structural teardowns of Ryan Trahan's
videos. Every file names its sources so the reasoning can be checked and so the
method can be updated when they publish something new.

**What is replicated is the method.** The ordering of decisions, the tests they
apply, the numbers they work to, the things they throw away.

**What is not replicated is the person.** No role prompts a model to be a named
individual or to write in their register. That is deliberate and it is not
squeamishness: asking for a personality produces an impression of a personality,
which is a worse output than the discipline that made the personality work in the
first place. The stair-stepping structure is transferable. Someone's voice is
not, and it is not ours to wear.

Practical version: the Ideas role starts with the title and thumbnail because
that is genuinely how that production works, not because it is doing a bit.

## Where the sources disagree

Two of them contradict each other outright on format lifespan, and the
disagreement is real rather than a misreading.

- **MrBeast's doc:** ditch formats while they still work, because audiences want
  new things and channels that rehash formats for years die.
- **Galloway:** when a format works, run it until they stop you, and do not
  abandon it because copycats appeared.

They are optimising different things. One is protecting a decade-old channel's
freshness; the other is maximising views on a format that currently works. At
Byron's audience size, Galloway's side wins: there is no format equity to protect
and no audience to bore. Revisit it the day a format becomes his signature.
`roles/02-packaging.md` carries the long version.

## The chain

Each role hands over a named artifact, which is what lets a failed run resume at
the role that failed rather than starting over.

| # | Produces | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `angle`, `title`, `thumbFrame`, `wow` | A stranger repeats it after one hearing |
| 2 | `hooks[]`, `winner`, `frames[]`, `expect` | Scroll stop, sound off, promise kept |
| 3 | `beats[]`, `words`, `constraint` | Every beat changes the situation |
| 4 | `script`, `wordCount`, `blocks` | Within 5% of budget, no useless sentences |
| 5 | `shots[]`, `background`, `clock` | Every shot names a real free source |
| 6 | `brief.json` → MP4 | Renders, and survives the grade |

The critic sits between 2 and 3: five hooks in, one out. It is the cheapest place
in the chain to fail, because a bad hook caught there costs nothing and caught
after render costs the whole render.
